1. OVERVIEW
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This release delivers a research benchmark of transformer-based NLP models for
detecting indicators of depression and anxiety from free-text discharge
summaries in the MIMIC-IV clinical database, together with explainability views
and a clinician-facing dashboard (demonstration). It is a research benchmark,
not a deployable clinical tool.

2. DELIVERED IN THIS RELEASE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Benchmarked models: ClinicalBERT, RoBERTa, DeBERTa, Clinical Longformer, and
  a locally-run open-weight model on RMIT RACE (no patient text sent to any
  external/commercial API).
- Dataset pipeline on MIMIC-IV: cohort aged 60+, labels derived from ICD-9/10
  diagnostic codes, patient-level train/validation/test split.
- Explainability: SHAP and attention-based views.
- Dashboard (local demo): data overview, model/performance comparison, and a
  live prediction demo.
- Documentation: research report, product backlog, risk register, issue
  register, sprint planning/review/retro notes.

3. KNOWN ISSUES / LIMITATIONS REMAINING IN THIS RELEASE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The following are known and accepted at the time of final release. Each is
tracked as a corresponding Bug issue in the Jira "Known Issues" sprint, so Jira
and this file match exactly.

  KI-1  [RMIT-96]   Moderate predictive performance. Benchmarked models reach
                    only moderate scores; the system is a research benchmark and
                    is NOT suitable for clinical deployment or autonomous use.

  KI-2  [RMIT-97]   Class imbalance. Depression/anxiety positives are minority
                    classes; recall on the positive class is lower than on the
                    majority class. F1 and recall are reported (not headline
                    accuracy) to keep this visible.

  KI-3  [RMIT-98]   512-token truncation. BERT-family models truncate long
                    discharge summaries at 512 tokens, dropping later content.
                    Clinical Longformer partially mitigates but does not fully
                    resolve this.

  KI-4  [RMIT-99]   Limited generalisability. Results are evaluated only on the
                    MIMIC-IV elderly (60+) US ICU cohort; performance on other
                    populations, hospitals or note styles is not established.

  KI-5  [RMIT-100]  Dashboard is a prototype/demo. The dashboard and live
                    prediction run locally and are illustrative only; they are
                    not production-hardened (no auth, scaling, or monitoring).

  KI-6  [RMIT-101]  No external clinical validation. Outputs are decision-support
                    only, not diagnostic, and have not been validated by
                    clinicians on unseen real-world data.

  KI-7  [RMIT-102]  Compute/credit constraints. RACE GPU credit and capacity
                    limits restricted some fine-tuning and hyperparameter
                    exploration; not all configurations were exhaustively tuned.

4. NOT IN SCOPE / FUTURE WORK
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- External clinical validation on unseen, real-world data.
- Production hardening and deployment of the dashboard.
- Broader cohorts (e.g. non-elderly, non-ICU, non-US) and additional models.
- Bias/fairness auditing across demographic subgroups.
